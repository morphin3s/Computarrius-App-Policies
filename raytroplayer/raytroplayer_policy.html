# RaytroPlayer Privacy Policy **Effective date:** November 19, 2026 **Developer:** Computarrius **Developer contact:** morphin3s@gmail.com **Privacy policy URL:** https://morphin3s.github.io/Computarrius-App-Policies/raytroplayer/raytroplayer_policy.html This Privacy Policy describes how **RaytroPlayer** (Android package `com.computarrius.raytroplayer`), an offline music player developed by Computarrius, accesses and processes information on your device. RaytroPlayer is designed to let you enjoy your own music without accounts, advertisements, subscriptions or unnecessary online services. ## 1. Data Collection and Sharing RaytroPlayer does not collect or transmit your personal data to the developer or to analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. Specifically: - No user account or registration is required. - No advertising or analytics SDKs are included. - No developer-operated online services are used. - No personal information, music files or listening history is uploaded to the developer. - Your data is not sold or shared with advertisers. - The application does not request Android's Internet permission. Music library information and application preferences are processed locally on your device. ## 2. Music and Folder Access With your permission, RaytroPlayer accesses audio files available through Android's media library. On Android 13 and later, the application requests permission to access audio media. On older supported Android versions, it requests the appropriate read permission for shared storage. The application reads available information about your music, including: - Song titles, artists and albums. - Track duration and file locations. - Available audio metadata and technical information. - Folder organization and file references. RaytroPlayer also allows you to select music folders using Android's system folder picker. The application stores selected folder addresses and persistent read permissions so those folders remain accessible after restarting or updating the application. Selected folders may remain accessible even when broad media-library permission is not granted. You can remove selected folders through **Manage Music Folders** or revoke access through Android settings. Removing a folder or playlist from RaytroPlayer does not delete the original audio files. Hidden folders are excluded from the application's library view. Hiding a folder does not delete its contents or revoke Android permissions. ## 3. Album Artwork RaytroPlayer reads embedded album artwork and artwork made available through Android's media provider. Artwork is decoded and temporarily cached in memory to display images in the music player, library, queue and home-screen widgets. RaytroPlayer does not: - Upload album artwork. - Search online artwork services. - Send artwork to developer-operated servers. ## 4. Local Preferences and Playback State RaytroPlayer stores the following information locally to preserve your preferences and restore playback: - Selected and hidden music folders. - Playlist names and song references. - Playback queue, song position and playback progress. - Shuffle and repeat settings. - Selected visual themes and custom colors. - Equalizer settings and presets. - Application preferences. - Permission-prompt and widget-display state. These records are stored in Android app-private storage and are not transmitted to the developer. Android's application security model isolates app-private storage from other ordinary applications. Temporary music-library and album-artwork caches are maintained in memory. Playlists contain references to local music files, not uploaded copies of the music. ## 5. Android Integration and Dependencies RaytroPlayer uses Android and AndroidX software libraries, including: - **Android Media3** for audio playback and media sessions. - **Jetpack Compose** for the application interface. - **Jetpack DataStore** for locally stored preferences. These components do not introduce advertising or analytics services into RaytroPlayer. Playback metadata and media controls may be made available to Android's media-session and notification systems and connected media controllers, including Bluetooth accessories. Home-screen widgets display playback information and album artwork through your device's launcher. These integrations support normal Android device functionality and do not constitute transmission of your music to developer-operated servers. ### Android Permissions RaytroPlayer uses permissions appropriate to its functionality: - **Audio/media access:** To discover and play local music. - **Folder access:** To read folders selected through Android's system picker. - **Foreground service:** To support background music playback. - **Modify audio settings:** To provide Equalizer functionality. - **Boot completed:** To refresh home-screen widgets after a device restart without automatically starting music playback. - **Wake lock:** Declared for playback support. - **Network state:** Declared by Media3; this permission does not independently grant Internet access. RaytroPlayer does not request location, microphone, camera, or unrestricted all-files access permissions. ## 6. External Applications and Services If you select a folder supplied by another application or document provider, that provider controls how its files are accessed and delivered. Some document providers may use network services independently of RaytroPlayer. When you open the public Privacy Policy link, Android may open the URL in an external web browser. Your browser, the privacy-policy hosting provider and external document providers operate according to their own privacy policies. Accessing an external website may disclose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to that website's hosting provider. RaytroPlayer does not receive this information from the browser or hosting provider. ## 7. Data Security RaytroPlayer is designed to minimize data exposure by processing music and application preferences locally. Application preferences and playback records are stored in Android app-private storage, protected by Android's application isolation mechanisms. The application does not transmit locally stored playback records, playlists, music metadata or preferences to developer-operated servers. Access to media files and selected folders remains subject to Android's permission system. No application can guarantee absolute security, but RaytroPlayer avoids unnecessary data collection and online transmission. ## 8. Data Retention, Removal and Updates Locally stored application data remains on your device until you modify or remove it, clear the application's storage, or uninstall RaytroPlayer. Normal in-place application updates generally preserve app-private data. RaytroPlayer disables Android backup and excludes its stored data from cloud backup and device transfer. Reinstalling the application should therefore not be relied upon to restore playlists, playback history or application preferences. ### Uninstalling RaytroPlayer Uninstalling the application removes its locally stored: - Playlists and queue records. - Themes and custom color preferences. - Equalizer settings. - Application preferences and widget state. - Persisted folder-access permissions. Uninstalling RaytroPlayer does **not** delete music files or embedded artwork stored outside the application's private storage. Clearing the application's storage through Android settings resets locally stored application data. Audio permissions can be managed through Android settings, and selected folder access can be managed through RaytroPlayer. Because the developer does not collect or retain your personal data on developer-operated servers, there is no developer-held personal-data account to delete. ## 9. Children's Privacy RaytroPlayer is a general-purpose local music player and is not specifically designed or directed toward children. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. ## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when RaytroPlayer's functionality, data processing or applicable requirements change. Any updated policy will be published at the public Privacy Policy URL. The effective date at the top of this document will be revised when the policy changes. ## 11.
